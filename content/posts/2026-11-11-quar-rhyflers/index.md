I shared the new Quar: Clash of Rhyfles starter set with a friend and kept the 12 Western Arnyaran Rhyflers minis. Can't wait to field my anteaters and play this WWI-inspired "grimsical" wargame. I painted them with a brown to white zenythal, speedpaints, oil washes and acrylic highlights.

Three stands of Auxilia Palatina, the first units for a new Late Imperial Western Roman army. The centre stand has a command group, with a cornicen and a draco standard bearer. I tried to paint shields using designs from the [Notitia Dignitatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notitia_Dignitatum), drawing from real Auxilia Palatina units from Ghaul. In particular:
- Honoriani Ascarii Seniores
- Salii Seniores
- Mattiaci Iuniores Gallicani
